## README overview

The new public **Seallo Connect** app adds Shopify installation-to-workspace linking and Shopify-hosted subscription plans while preserving the existing standalone Shopify integration. See Seallo Connect setup, billing, quotas and deployment and mandatory privacy workflows.

Multi-store cash-on-delivery commerce platform for Morocco. Morea is the reference store: luxury modest sportswear storefront + COD admin.

**Storefront:** fashion-house UX (campaign homepage, editorial PDP, restrained blush).

**Store admin:** dense and operational — not luxurious.

**Platform:** SaaS owner home for super admins (all tenants).

Stores are subdomain tenants. Each store has its own catalog, staff, orders, and Sendit/Discord credentials.

Merchants create an account at `/signup` without administrator approval: enter a business name and email, follow a verification link, then choose a password. A verified account opens its workspace overview immediately. New workspaces begin unpublished and require an active subscription before operational pages, writes, agent APIs or connected-order intake can be used. Existing workspaces retain their access policy. Password recovery is available from sign-in. See self-service signup and subscription setup.
