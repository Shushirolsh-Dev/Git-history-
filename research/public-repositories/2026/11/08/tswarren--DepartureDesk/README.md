## README overview

DepartureDesk is an operations and financial-management application for travel agencies that plan, sell, and operate group travel.

The application is intended to connect four views of the same departure without collapsing them into one record:

1. What the agency has arranged or guaranteed with each supplier.

3. Which travelers are participating and who is financially responsible.

4. How reservations, client receipts, supplier obligations, and supplier payments progress over time.

The shipped domain is agency identity plus the complete M1 Client and Supplier directories: an agency workspace, agency users, invitations, sessions, offices as context, administrator maintenance of the profile, offices, and users, Client Person, Client Organization, person-backed and organization-backed Client, person-owned and organization-owned contact points including organization websites, effective-dated organization contacts, Supplier with fixed categories and Supplier-owned contact points including websites, Supplier Locations, Supplier Contacts, and Supplier Contact-owned email and phone destinations. M1E shipped directory proof and hardening without a new domain aggregate. M2 is complete: dated Departures, activation, `D-` issuance, search, departed transitions, scheduled departed jobs, schedule/currency/lifecycle corrections, and M2C proof and hardening. Travel Program remains deferred. M3A ships draft Supplier Arrangement structure under Departures. M3B ships draft Item capacity configuration and the capacity event/projection/reconciliation engine without Arrangement activation or Staff event UI.
